# Tamil Nadu Entrepreneurs Forum — scroll story

Same system as the Salem story (`story/STORYBOARD.md`): **6 keyframe stills → 6 clips**, one
continuous camera move scrubbed by scroll. Clip _n_ starts on K_n_ and ends on K_n+1_.

Theme: **"One state. Every industry."** — a journey across Tamil Nadu's industrial cities:
the Cauvery delta at dawn → Chennai's car plants → Tiruppur's knitwear floors →
a Chettinad merchant courtyard (the state's historic trading community) → Thoothukudi/Chennai port
at night → your seat. It is the forum for every city that is not Salem or Coimbatore.

Generated files go in `story/tamilnadu/keyframes/K1.png … K6.png`, clips in `story/tamilnadu/clips/01.mp4 … 06.mp4`.

## Look (append to every image prompt — identical to Salem)

> Photorealistic cinematic still, shot on ARRI Alexa 35 with a 35mm anamorphic lens,
> natural light, Kodak Portra 400 colour grade, deep navy shadows and warm brass-gold
> highlights, subtle film grain, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos, no watermarks.

Realism rules: no close-up faces, no readable signage, no recognisable landmarks or brand names;
people appear as hands, silhouettes, backs or soft-focus groups.
**Composition rule:** keep the main subject inside the centre third — mobile shows a centre 3:4 crop.
